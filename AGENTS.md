# LOT06 Project Guidance

## Design-document entry point

- For work that affects gameplay, narrative, levels, interaction, environment art, audio, scope, or playtesting, read `docs/gdd/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` before proposing or making a change.
- If the task depends on detailed rules, sequence, timing, or asset scope, also read `docs/gdd/LOT06_GDD.md`.
- `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` is the short, new-chat briefing. `LOT06_GDD.md` is the local full snapshot of the approved Notion GDD.
- Treat items marked **미정** or **첫 그레이박스 테스트 뒤 결정** as unresolved. Do not present them as implemented or approved requirements.
- The Notion GDD remains the canonical team document. Do not modify it unless the user explicitly asks. When its approved content changes, update both local GDD files in the same change and refresh their sync date.
- Do not add private Notion links, credentials, or personal information to repository documents.
